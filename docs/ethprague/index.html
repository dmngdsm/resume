You Can't Automate Judgment: Taste as the Missing Skill in Web3 | Talk at ETH Prague 2026

AI has collapsed execution. Anyone can ship, fork, or generate fast. What hasn’t scaled is judgment. That gap is where taste lives. This talk reframes taste as a practical skill, not a vague aesthetic trait: the ability to recognize what fits, what’s excessive, and what actually matters. I gave this talk at ETH Prague 2026.

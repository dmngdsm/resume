# Temporary personal page

White background, near-black oversized sans-serif prose, muted gray emphasis, underlined inline links. No images, navigation menu, cards or custom font downloads. Work and role take precedence over a portrait.

Small name and location at the top. Three short paragraphs introduce Ivan, his current role at Ambire, and an invitation to connect by email. A quiet footer notes the forthcoming portfolio and links to social profiles.

Use fluid typography, generous whitespace, 24px mobile gutters, visible keyboard focus and semantic HTML. Minimum text size 12px. Verify at 375px and 1440px.

Local motion experiment: a saturated electric blue (#2B47F2) pointer trail with a dense center and feathered edges inspired by https://sundaymedia.co/. Feathered strokes, with the responsive brush radius reduced by 20%, and fine, stationary noise appear behind the typography, linger briefly and dissolve back into white. Each brush sample expires after 0.7 seconds; the canvas is cleared and redrawn every frame so no residual outlines accumulate. Grain uses lighter and darker saturated blue tones. Grain is composited only into the existing paint alpha, so it fades with the stroke and never textures the white background. The paint layer uses 90% opacity for a vivid blue stroke behind the text and never intercepts links or selection. All text responds word by word: words turn white when the dense blue trail covers their center, then return to their original black or gray as it fades. Link underlines also turn white when covered. Detection follows the brush opacity, not just pointer hover; keyboard focus outlines remain visible. Finger swipes paint on mobile through passive touch listeners, preserving native scrolling, pinch zoom and link taps. Reduced-motion users see the still page. The animation pauses when the tab is hidden or the window loses focus, clears on resize, and uses no additional dependencies. Touch scrolling keeps the active trail visible.

Header interaction: The header defaults to “Ivan Manchev”. Every 7 seconds (from reveal start to reveal start), it scrambles to “Web3, AI, Creative direction”, holds the completed phrase for 1.5 seconds, then scrambles back to the name. Mouse hover or visible keyboard focus reveals the interests and pauses the timer; leaving resumes the rotation after 7 seconds. Touch taps reveal the phrase for the same 1.5-second hold (a second tap closes it) and restart the 7-second schedule. Automatic rotation pauses while the tab is hidden. Reserve the longer label width to prevent shifting; the header wraps on narrow screens. Scramble runs for 650ms, cancels cleanly on reversal, and switches immediately with reduced motion. Assistive technology gets a stable heading label.

Production motion: two independently swimming koi-like blue brush strokes appear by default. Use `/?koi=0` for a pointer-only comparison. Indistinct washes of electric blue suggest fish through their swimming motion. Fuller, shorter bodies blend into compact, faint tail fans, avoiding both sharp fin outlines and long filament-like tails. Translucent brush samples keep the edges hazy. They retain the same grain and white-text response, with coverage accounting for the softer brush and transparency. They follow slow looping paths without needing pointer input. The koi and finger-painted trail work on touch screens, scale to the viewport, pause in hidden tabs and respect reduced motion. No new dependency.

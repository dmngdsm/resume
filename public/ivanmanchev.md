## Biography

Ivan began his career in 2011 as a copywriter at the Bulgarian advertising agency Archer Ideas. In 2013, he joined All Channels Communications, one of Bulgaria's largest communications groups. His work there earned a Bulgarian advertising award in 2014.

He then moved into entrepreneurship, cofounding a small game development studio with two friends to build an online strategy game. He handled marketing, distribution and fundraising, while helping shape the game and the studio's direction. In 2015, the business expanded into boutique software development, where he focused on business development for client projects.

Ivan became interested in crypto in late 2016 through the emerging market for initial coin offerings (ICOs). After working on marketing for ICO projects, he cofounded a crypto marketing agency with industry partners in early 2017. The agency worked with several crypto projects before closing in late 2018 as the bear market sharply reduced marketing budgets.

In 2019, he joined AdEx Network as a communications specialist. In 2021, he was part of the team that conceived Ambire Wallet, building on infrastructure already developed for AdEx. The company raised a $3 million seed round, and Ivan became closely involved in the wallet's launches, announcements, campaigns and events.

Since stepping into the CMO role in 2023, he has led marketing across Ambire and AdEx Network. His experience spans agency copywriting, building businesses and bringing Web3 products to market, combining creative judgment with hands-on execution.
